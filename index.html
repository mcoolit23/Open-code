About Me

I am a dedicated hospitality professional with a passion for delivering exceptional housekeeping standards. Over the past six years, I have built my career in Finland's dynamic hotel industry, developing expertise in team leadership, operational management, and quality assurance.

My experience spans managing large, multicultural housekeeping teams, implementing efficient cleaning protocols, and maintaining the highest standards of cleanliness and guest satisfaction. I believe that exceptional housekeeping is the foundation of any successful hotel operation.
