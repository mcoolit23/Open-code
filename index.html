About

Practical leadership, grounded in hotel operations.

I want my team to know they can come to me, count on my support, and enjoy working together, even on busy days.

I set clear priorities for the housekeeping team and adapt the plan when hotel demand or staff availability changes. I use quality checks and guest feedback to spot recurring issues, then work with the team to improve how we handle them.

I work to create a positive, lively environment where people feel comfortable asking for help or raising a concern. When I take care of the team and give them the support they need, they can take pride in their work and give guests better service.

My work connected to Scandic Hotels in Finland has given me a close view of hotel housekeeping in a demanding operating environment. I am interested in continuing that work in leadership roles where quality, teamwork, and continuous improvement are treated as part of the daily routine.
