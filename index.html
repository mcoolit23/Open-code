About

Practical leadership, grounded in hotel operations.

Housekeeping is a people role and an operations role. The best results come from clear priorities, consistent standards, and teams that feel supported enough to solve problems early.

My experience sits at the point where guest expectations meet daily operations. Over six years in Finland's hospitality sector, I have worked with housekeeping priorities, room readiness, team coordination, and the routines that help a property deliver a consistent standard.

I approach housekeeping management as both a people role and an operations role. Teams need clear expectations, practical support, and the confidence to raise problems early. Operations need structure: defined priorities, reliable checks, and communication that keeps departments aligned.

My work connected to Scandic Hotels in Finland has given me a close view of hotel housekeeping in a demanding operating environment. I am interested in continuing that work in leadership roles where quality, teamwork, and continuous improvement are treated as part of the daily routine.
